About

I build AI systems that actually work. My world orbits around Agentic AI, GenAI architectures, and production-grade multi-agent workflows. Started in the trenches of collaborative robotics and life sciences, evolved into orchestrating complex AI pipelines, and now I'm obsessed with making intelligent agents do useful things at scale.

By day, I'm designing GenAI systems, wrestling with agent orchestration, and thinking about planning-execution workflows. By night (and weekends), you'll find me diving into technical writing, running workshops, and publishing research across AI/ML. I'm the type who gets genuinely excited about reinforcement learning theory and then gets even more excited about actually implementing it in the real world.

Outside of work, I'm either swimming laps to clear my head, deep in a PS4 gaming session (procrastination fuel), making music, or—let's be honest—just thinking about AI from a different angle. The reading never stops, and neither does the curiosity.
